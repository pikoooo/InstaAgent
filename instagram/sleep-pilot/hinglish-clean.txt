Neend nahi aati? Apni dinner, phone aur wake-up ki habits check kijiye.
Agar aapko achchi sleep chahiye, toh raat ke routine ke saath subah ka routine bhi important hai. Chaliye 3 simple habits samajhte hain.
Number 1: Dinner aur bedtime ke beech thoda gap rakhiye. Sone se bilkul pehle heavy meal avoid karna aapki sleep routine ke liye helpful ho sakta hai.
Aur agar aapko raat mein acidity ya reflux hota hai, toh letne se kam se kam 3 ghante pehle meal lena symptoms mein madad kar sakta hai.
Number 2: Roz lagbhag ek hi time par sone aur uthne ki koshish kijiye, weekends par bhi. Saath hi, sleep ke liye enough time zaroor rakhiye.
Number 3: Sone se 30 minutes pehle phone aur doosre electronic devices band karne ki koshish kijiye. Late-night scrolling se wind down karna mushkil ho sakta hai.
Phone ki bright light bhi aapko alert rakh sakti hai. Aur bed mein scrolling ya kaam karne ke bajaye, bed ko sleep se associate karne ki habit banaiye.
Yeh habits helpful ho sakti hain, lekin sleep problems baar-baar ho rahi hain, toh healthcare professional se baat kijiye. Is video ko save kar lijiye.
